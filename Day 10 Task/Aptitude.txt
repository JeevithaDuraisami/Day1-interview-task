 
BLOCK 1 – APTITUDE (Speed, Time & Distance)

Question 1
A car travels 60 km in 1 hour. What is the speed?
Solution:
      Distance=60 km
      Time=1hr
      speed=distance/Time
           =60/1
           =60.
Answer: speed=60km.

Question 2
A train travels at 50 km/hr. How long will it take to travel 150 km?
Solution: 
     Distance=150km
     Speed=50km
     time=distance/time
         =150/50
         =3
Answer: 3 hours.


Question 3
If a person walks at 5 km/hr, how far will they walk in 3 hours?
Soluion:
      Distance=speed * time 
         Speed=5km/hr
         Time=3 hours
         Distance=5 * 3
                =15km
Answer: distance=15km

Question 4
Two trains move in opposite direction at 40 km/hr and 60 km/hr.
Find relative speed.
Solution: 
   Oppesite direction= s1+s2
   Relative speed=s1+s2
                 =40+60
                 =100km/hr
Answer: Relative Speed=100km/hr.

Question 5
A car travels first half distance at 40 km/hr and second half at 60 km/hr.
Find average speed.
Solution:
 Average Speed=(2 * s1 * s2)/s1+s2
              =(2 * 40 * 60)/40+ 60
              =(4800)/100
              =48 km/hr
              
Answer: Average Speed= 48 km/hr.
