BLOCK 1 – APTITUDE (Mixtures & Averages)

Question 1
The average of 5 numbers is 20. Find the total sum.
Solution:
     Formula:
         Average=Total Sum/Total numbers
             20=Total Sum/5
             20*5=Total Sum
             100=Total Sum
Answer: Total Sum:100

Question 2
The average of 10 numbers is 15. If one number is removed, the average becomes 14. Find the removed number.
Solution:
       10 =Total sum/15
        10*15=totalsum
        Totalsum=150

    one number is removed then average becomes is 14
       9*14=Totalsum
       126=Totalsum
      removed totalsum =126
removed number=totalsum-removed Totalsum
              =150-126
              =24
Answer:removed number:24


Question 3
A container has milk and water in ratio 3:2. If 10 liters of water is added, ratio becomes 3:4. Find initial quantity.
Solution:
     milk=3x
     water=2x 
     added for water 10 liters=2x+10
     ratio=3:4
     3x/2x+10=3/4
     3x*(4)=(3)*(2x+10)
     12x=6x+30
     12x-6x=30
     6x=30
     x=30/6
     x=5
initial quantity 3:2
          = 3(x):2(x)
          =3(5)+2(5)
          =15+10
          =125
Answer:Initialy quantity:25
